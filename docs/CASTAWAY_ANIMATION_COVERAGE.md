# Castaway animation coverage

Generated from `assets/castaway/castaway_clips.json`, the wiring table in `src/human.js` (`coverage.declare`, `coverage.declareUpper`) and the playback seen in long
simulated runs of the real game. **Loaded is not integrated.** A clip counts as *integrated* only when a behaviour of the castaway's own mind can trigger it and it has been
seen playing in the game during **autonomous** play. Clips forced from the console, and quests forced with `life.dev.force`, are recorded as forced and never counted.
Not yet checked by eye for every clip: foot sliding at gait changes, hand contact on every prop, and the canoe and seated heights.

## What drives the clips now

The castaway's mind (`castaway_mind.js`) chooses an *activity*; `human.js` runs it and `clipFor` maps what the activity is doing to a clip. A new activity, **quest**
(`castaway_life.js`), runs goals that come from his own drives. A quest's steps carry their clip names and are listed here by quest kind:

| Quest | Clips it plays (in order) |
|---|---|
| collect | walk_start > walk > (turn_*) > look_curious > kneel_down > kneel_idle (pick up) > kneel_rise > fish_inspect_catch (examining it) > ... > kneel_down > kneel_idle (store) > kneel_rise > contemplation or excitement |
| investigate | idle_look_around > walk_start > walk > (observe_animal or birdwatch on the way) > look_curious > track_animals > kneel_down > kneel_idle > kneel_rise > fish_inspect_catch (a specimen) > contemplation or look_up_sky |
| adventure | staff_pickup > walk_staff_start > walk_staff > (observe_animal) > high_lookout or contemplation > look_down_from_height > shout > look_curious > wave_hello or celebrate |
| create | walk > contemplation > kneel_down > kneel_idle (building the arrangement, part by part) > kneel_rise > contemplation > clap or nod_yes |
| experiment | walk > kneel_down > kneel_idle > kneel_rise > excitement, or head_scratch or shrug |
| build | walk > contemplation > kneel_down > kneel_idle > kneel_rise > contemplation > sit_down > sit_idle (on the bench) or nod_yes |
| beachplay | walk > play_kick_sand / play_flex_pose / play_skip > laugh_hearty |
| track | walk (slow) > track_animals, then the rat hunt |

## Milestones (what each one connects)

- **Milestone A: Core movement** (run starts and cuts). Base clips still unconnected: 3 (`run_start`, `run_cut_left`, `run_cut_right`).
- **Milestone B: Everyday survival** (rising, lounging, coconut pickup, food preparation, rain). Base clips still unconnected: 8 (`stand_up`, `lounge_sit_back`, `lounge_recline`, `coconut_pickup`, `rest_on_rock`, `rise_from_rock`, `prepare_food`, `shelter_from_rain`).
- **Milestone C: Fishing and cooking** (catch outcomes, spear aim and recover, turning fish on the fire). Base clips still unconnected: 7 (`turn_fish_on_fire`, `fish_lift_catch`, `fish_celebrate`, `fish_fail`, `spear_aim`, `spear_recover_miss`, `spear_ground_recover`).
- **Milestone D: Environmental exploration** (canoe extras, climbing, boulders, trees, balance, swimming once the water clips are fixed). Base clips still unconnected: 17 (`canoe_sit_down`, `canoe_paddle_left`, `canoe_paddle_hard`, `canoe_drift`, `canoe_pull_on_land`, `tree_climb_up`, `tree_climb_down`, `tree_hold_rest`, `reach_for_handhold`, `climb_approach_tree`, `climb_onto_boulder`, `climb_down_boulder`, `step_down_rock`, `clamber_rocks`, `balance_uneven`, `swim_crawl`, `tread_water`).
- **Milestone E: Wildlife and expression** (approaching animals, reactions, play). Base clips still unconnected: 4 (`play_toss_catch`, `startled`, `approach_carefully`, `run_look_back`).
- **Milestone F: Layering and complete coverage** (gesture base clips and the remaining layers, rare reactions). Base clips still unconnected: 1 (`embarrassed_recover`).

Milestones A to E of the original plan are now partly done: A (movement starts, stops, turns, staff pickup and lean), B (rest, sleep and wake sequences, drinking, coconut), C (fishing outcomes
still wait for the fishing branch), D (a first set of climbing look-outs from high ground; real climbs, canoe and swimming remain), E (observing, tracking, reactions as gestures) and F (face controls, 24 layered
gestures) all have working first steps. See the table below for each clip.

## Face controls

`castaway_face.json` defines 19 controls and 10 visemes. They are applied after `mixer.update()` (bones reset to rest first), eased in and out, with an automatic blink every 2.5 to 6.5 s. Presets in use: neutral, friendly (strength follows happiness), sun_squint (bright midday, outdoors),
sad (very unhappy), sleepy (asleep), surprise (running from a boar), laugh (laughing clips), grin (dancing and play), whistle (while whistling), frown (frustration), and a custom curious brow for quest steps. Visemes (speech) are not used.
