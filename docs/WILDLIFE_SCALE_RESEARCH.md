# Paradise Island wildlife scale research

Target rule (from the owner's brief): every creature is `real adult dimension x 1.5`, with the same factor on every axis.
The castaway is exactly 2.00 m (measured: body mesh 1.843 m at scale 1.0, scaled by 2.00 / 1.843 = 1.0854), and is the
fixed reference. The 1.5x factor is never applied to the castaway.

Each entry: scientific name, the biological reference used, the sources, the selected typical adult value, and the
game target (x1.5). Juvenile and sex notes are given where the sources give them. Uncertain values are marked.

## Monkey

### Tufted capuchin, Sapajus apella (game model: "Capuchin monkey", a Blender asset built at real scale)
- Reference used: head-body length 0.46 m, tail 0.49 m, weight about 2.6-4.8 kg (Guianan subspecies S. a. apella).
- Sources: Pierre Wildlife, S. a. apella profile (https://pierrewildlife.com/?p=44547): body 46 cm, tail 49 cm, weight 4.8 kg,
  males larger than females. Worldspecies table on the large-headed capuchin (https://worldspecies.org/ntaxa/915090): snout-to-vent 49 cm,
  weight 2.643 kg. The Animal Diversity Web account was not retrieved.
- Selected typical adult: head-body 0.46 m. Adult variation about +-8% in length (weight 2.6-4.8 kg is a 1.8x spread; length varies less).
- Game target (x1.5): head-body 0.69 m. The asset is already at real scale (workshop docs: 0.46 m nose-to-rump), so the rig
  scale is 1.5. The previous session used 1.7 (0.78 m body): corrected to 1.5.
- Individual variation: adults 0.92-1.08 of the target (narrower than the old 0.82-1.3).

## Wild boar, Sus scrofa
- Reference used: adult shoulder height 0.80 m (typical; males about 0.75-0.80 m in Europe, females about 0.70 m), head-body
  length 1.40 m (range 1.00-1.70 m), tail 0.16-0.30 m.
- Sources: UK wildlife site (https://www.wildlifeonline.me.uk/animals/species/wild-boar): shoulder 70-100 cm, body 100-170 cm
  (average 140-150 cm), tail 16-30 cm. Ultimate Ungulate (https://ultimateungulate.com/Artiodactyla/Sus_scrofa.html): shoulder
  55-110 cm, body 90-200 cm. European educational figures: males 75-80 cm shoulder, body 150 cm; large NE Asian males 110-118 cm.
- Selected typical adult: shoulder 0.80 m, head-body 1.40 m. Males 5-10% larger than females (sources).
- Game target (x1.5): shoulder 1.20 m, head-body 2.10 m.
- Piglet: NOT YET RESEARCHED (see open list).

## Polynesian rat, Rattus exulans
- Reference used: head-body length 0.11 m (range 0.08-0.14 m), tail 0.13 m (range 0.11-0.16 m; about equal to body), weight 0.06 kg (range 0.03-0.10 kg).
- Sources: Western Australia DPIRD Pacific rat pest alert (https://www.agric.wa.gov.au/node/1818): head-body 8-14 cm, weight 30-100 g,
  tail 125-135 mm. Queensland government profile (https://www.business.qld.gov.au/industries/farms-fishing-forestry/agriculture/land-management/health-pests-weeds-diseases/pests/invasive-animals/other/pacific-rat):
  body 80-140 mm, tail 108-147 mm. Ecology Asia (https://ecologyasia.com/verts/mammals/polynesian-rat.htm): up to 14 cm, 65 g.
  Museum study skins from Manono (Samoa), total length 235 mm, tail 127 mm, weights 34-38 g.
- Game target (x1.5): head-body 0.165 m, tail 0.195 m.

## Pacific boa, Candoia bibroni
- Reference used: adult total length 1.0 m (sex-averaged midpoint of the range 0.6-1.5 m; females larger).
- Sources: Wikipedia Candoia bibroni (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candoia_bibroni): adults up to about 1.5 m total length. Wikipedia
  C. b. australis (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candoia_bibroni_australis): 0.6-1.5 m, females larger. Genus range 0.6-1.8 m
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candoia) is not species-specific.
- Choice: 1.0 m is a judgement (midpoint). The owner's open notes want giant snakes; that is the owner's call, not this task.
- Game target (x1.5): 1.5 m total length.

## Barn owl, Tyto alba
- Reference used: total length 0.36 m (range 0.32-0.40 m), wingspan 0.95 m (range 0.80-1.25 m), weight 0.40 kg (range 0.25-0.63 kg; females heavier).
- Sources: Global summary (https://dimensions.com/element/barn-owl-tyto-alba, quoted in several profiles): length 32-40 cm, wingspan 80-110 cm,
  weight 0.25-0.57 kg. UK Wildlife Trusts (https://www.wildlifetrusts.org/wildlife-explorer/birds/birds-prey/barn-owl): 33-39 cm, wingspan 89 cm, weight 300 g.
  Connecticut DEEP (https://test-cm.ct.gov/deep/wildlife/fact-sheets/barn-owl): males 13-15 in, females 14-20 in long; wingspans 41-47 in.
  Owl Research Institute (https://www.owlresearchinstitute.org/barn-owl): males 440-510 g, females 510-625 g, wingspans 100-125 cm.
  Cornell Birds of the World species account was not retrieved (paywalled).
- Selected typical adult: length 0.36 m, wingspan 0.95 m, weight 0.40 kg.
- Game target (x1.5): length 0.54 m, wingspan 1.43 m.

## Greater flamingo, Phoenicopterus roseus
- Reference used: body length 1.20 m (range 1.10-1.50 m), standing height 1.30 m (range 1.10-1.50 m), wingspan 1.55 m (range 1.40-1.87 m).
- Sources: Birds of the World identification (https://birdsoftheworld.org/bow/species/grefla3/cur/identification): body 120-125 cm, wingspan 140-187 cm.
  Zoo profile (https://zoo.kyiv.ua/en/greater-flamingo/): body 110-150 cm, wingspan 140-165 cm, males 2.5-4 kg, females 2-3 kg.
  Standing height 1.1-1.5 m (https://ranchotexaslanzarote.com/en/animales/greater-flamingo/ and others). The standing height figure is weakly sourced: flagged.
- Game target (x1.5): body length 1.80 m, standing height 1.95 m, wingspan 2.33 m.
